The Occoquan River & the Town of Occoquan

The Occoquan River has been a vital waterway for thousands of years. Indigenous peoples relied on the river for transportation, fishing, and trade long before European settlement. During the American Revolution, flour milled along the Occoquan supplied General George Washington’s Continental Army.
