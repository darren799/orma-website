The Occoquan River

The name Occoquan is derived from an Algonquian word meaning “at the end of the water,” reflecting the river’s historic role as a gathering place for travel, trade, and community.

2026 Blessing of the Fleet

In recognition of America’s 250th Anniversary, the Blessing of the Fleet serves as a moment of remembrance, reflection, and commitment to safety on the water.

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Location: Occoquan River, Occoquan, Virginia
  • Open To: Recreational and commercial vessels, families, and the public
