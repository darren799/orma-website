2026 ORMA Blessing of the Fleet

The Occoquan River Maritime Association Blessing of the Fleet is a time-honored maritime tradition that brings together boaters, families, clergy, and the wider community to offer prayers for safety, protection, and good fortune for all who work or recreate on the water.

Rooted in centuries-old seafaring customs, the Blessing of the Fleet honors the deep connection between faith, water, and maritime life. On the Occoquan River, this ceremony reflects the region’s long history as a working waterway that supported trade, industry, and ultimately the founding of the United States.

2026 Event Details

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Location: Occoquan River, Occoquan, Virginia
  • Participants: Recreational boats, commercial vessels, kayaks, and paddlecraft
  • Open To: Boaters, families, visitors, and the public

What to Expect

  • A ceremonial blessing offered by local clergy
  • Vessels passing in review to receive the blessing
  • Community gathering along the riverfront
  • Recognition of maritime heritage and service

America 250 Significance

The 2026 Blessing of the Fleet holds special meaning as part of America’s Semiquincentennial. During the American Revolution, the Occoquan River supplied flour and provisions to General George Washington’s Continental Army. This event honors that legacy by recognizing the river’s role in sustaining both local livelihoods and national independence.

By blessing today’s vessels, ORMA symbolically connects modern mariners with generations who relied on the Occoquan River in service to community, country, and freedom.
