About ORMA

The Occoquan River Maritime Association (ORMA) is a community-based organization that brings together boaters, marinas, yacht clubs, public safety agencies, and residents in support of stewardship, safety, and maritime heritage.

2026 Blessing of the Fleet

The Blessing of the Fleet honors maritime tradition while reflecting on America’s 250th Anniversary and the generations who have lived, worked, and traveled along the Occoquan River.

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Location: Belmont Bay Harbor Marina Gas Dock, 570 Harbor Side St, Woodbridge, VA 22191 (38.6550, -77.2329)
  • Open To: Recreational and commercial vessels, families, and the public
