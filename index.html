The Occoquan River

Flowing through the heart of the community, the Occoquan River has long connected people, commerce, and culture. Its story spans generations and continues to shape the region’s maritime traditions.

Learn more about the history of the Occoquan River

About the Occoquan River Maritime Association (ORMA)

The Occoquan River Maritime Association (ORMA) is a community-based organization that brings together boaters, marinas, yacht clubs, public safety agencies, and residents who share a connection to the Occoquan River.

ORMA promotes cooperation, stewardship, boating safety, and respect for the river while supporting events and traditions that reflect the region’s maritime heritage.

2026 Blessing of the Fleet

In recognition of America’s 250th Anniversary, the Blessing of the Fleet serves as a moment of remembrance and reflection—honoring generations connected to the river and reaffirming a shared commitment to safety and community on the water.

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Location: Occoquan River, Occoquan, Virginia
  • Open To: Recreational and commercial vessels, families, and the public
