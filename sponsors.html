Why Sponsor

Sponsorship support helps ensure the safety, accessibility, and continuity of the Blessing of the Fleet while commemorating America’s 250th Anniversary. Sponsors support community engagement, maritime tradition, and stewardship of the Occoquan River.

Sponsorship Levels

  • Community Sponsor — General event support
  • Maritime Sponsor — Safety and stewardship support
  • Steward Sponsor — Accessibility and educational support
  • Founding Sponsor — Core event logistics and coordination
