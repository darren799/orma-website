The History of the Occoquan River

The Occoquan River has been a vital waterway in Northern Virginia for centuries. From Indigenous use to colonial trade and shipbuilding, it remains a defining feature of the region’s maritime heritage.

The Blessing of the Fleet

The Blessing of the Fleet is a centuries-old maritime tradition offering prayers for safety, calm seas, and prosperity for vessels and their crews.
