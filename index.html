The History of the Occoquan River

The Occoquan River has served as a vital waterway in Northern Virginia for centuries. From its early use by Indigenous peoples, to its role in colonial trade, milling, and shipbuilding, the river has long been a lifeline for the surrounding communities.

During the 18th and 19th centuries, the Town of Occoquan flourished as a commercial port, exporting grain, lumber, and goods throughout the region. Today, the river remains a treasured natural and cultural resource.

The Blessing of the Fleet

The Blessing of the Fleet is a centuries-old maritime tradition offering prayers for safety, calm seas, and prosperity for vessels and crews.

On the Occoquan River, this event unites boaters, clergy, families, and the community in honoring our shared maritime heritage.
