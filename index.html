The Occoquan River

The name Occoquan is derived from an Algonquian word commonly interpreted as “at the end of the water,” referring to the river’s fall line where navigable waters meet the rapids.

Over centuries, the Occoquan River has connected Indigenous communities, colonial industry, Revolutionary-era supply networks, and today’s maritime life.

About the Occoquan River Maritime Association (ORMA)

The Occoquan River Maritime Association (ORMA) is a community-driven organization rooted in a shared connection to the Occoquan River and the people who live, work, and recreate along its waters.

ORMA brings together boaters, marinas, yacht clubs, public safety partners, and local residents to encourage cooperation, stewardship, and respect for the river.

2026 Blessing of the Fleet

In the context of America’s 250th Anniversary, the Blessing of the Fleet serves as a moment of remembrance and reflection—honoring those connected to the river across generations while reaffirming a shared commitment to safety and community on the water.

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Location: Occoquan River, Occoquan, Virginia
  • Open To: Recreational and commercial vessels, families, and the public
