About the Blessing of the Fleet

The ORMA Blessing of the Fleet is a traditional maritime ceremony offering prayers for safety, protection, and prosperity for all who travel, work, or recreate on the water. Rooted in centuries-old seafaring customs, the blessing reflects the deep bond between community, faith, and maritime life.

On the Occoquan River, this ceremony carries special significance. During the American Revolution, flour milled along the Occoquan supplied General George Washington’s Continental Army, making the river a quiet but essential contributor to American independence.

2026 Event Details

  • Date: Saturday, May 16, 2026
  • Location: Occoquan River, Occoquan, Virginia
  • Who May Participate: Powerboats, sailboats, kayaks, paddlecraft
  • Open To: Boaters, families, visitors, and the public

America 250 Significance

The 2026 Blessing of the Fleet is held in recognition of America’s Semiquincentennial. By blessing today’s vessels, ORMA honors the generations of mariners, millers, and river communities who supported the birth of the United States through their labor and service.
