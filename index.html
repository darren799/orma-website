The Occoquan River

The name Occoquan is derived from an Algonquian word commonly interpreted as “at the end of the water,” referring to the river’s fall line where navigable waters meet the rapids. This location has long served as a natural gathering place for travel, trade, and community.

For centuries, the Occoquan River has connected Indigenous peoples, colonial industry, Revolutionary-era logistics, and today’s vibrant maritime community.
